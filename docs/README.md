# docs

Organised as the paper is: the question, the split, the two schemes, the results, what composition is, and what it costs.
**[PLAN_Z.md](PLAN_Z.md)** is the one active plan. Superseded plans are in [history/](history/).

## The question and the split

- [THE_TASK.md](THE_TASK.md): the task. One example in, new instances out, stored cheaply, nothing damaged.
- [SPLIT.md](SPLIT.md): the compositional split. The 40 training tasks expose the parts; every test task is graded by what it
  leaves unseen (G1 relation, G2 binding, G3 scene, G4 part), and every result is stratified by grade.

## The two schemes

- [METHOD.md](METHOD.md): the **supervised scheme**. Concepts are read from the instruction (object, instance, goal). Held
  concepts are pointers to the policy's own fields; new ones are fitted prototypes and task leaves; practice chooses sources and
  operators.
- [UNSUPERVISED.md](UNSUPERVISED.md): the **unsupervised scheme**. Units are cut at the demonstration's contact events and
  named by the policy's likelihood; pointer programs run with no instruction grammar.

## Results

- [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md): every read, in the order it ran, with its ledger tags; audited (`experiments/audit_numbers.py`).
- [LIBERO_PRO.md](LIBERO_PRO.md): the second benchmark, in our framing.
- [VALIDITY.md](VALIDITY.md): validity checks (generated by `experiments/make_validity_doc.py`).

## What composition is

- [INTERPRETATION.md](INTERPRETATION.md): what the units and operators donate. Success ≈ grasp competence × place competence;
  composition in time, not superposition; operators by architecture.
- [COBWEB_UPDATE.md](COBWEB_UPDATE.md): the theory the consolidation rests on (Propositions 1–7).
- [GRAMMAR.md](GRAMMAR.md): the qualifier grammar (generated by `experiments/make_grammar_doc.py`).

## What composition costs

- [COST.md](COST.md): accuracy, space and time of every method (generated by `experiments/h2_cost.py`, `h2_latency.py`).
- [SCALING.md](SCALING.md): storage and compute against the number of tasks (generated by `experiments/h3_scaling.py`).
- [FIT_TIME.md](FIT_TIME.md): why a unit took 39 minutes, and the fast fit (the frozen prefix cached; 200 steps).

## Related work

- [RELATED.md](RELATED.md): baselines and related work.
- [PLAN_X.md](PLAN_X.md): the framework beyond LIBERO (CALVIN, Bridge via SimplerEnv, EPIC-KITCHENS-100).
- [EGODEX.md](EGODEX.md): X3 stage 1: EgoDex inventory, the graded split, contact events, the GR00T recipe, evaluation.
