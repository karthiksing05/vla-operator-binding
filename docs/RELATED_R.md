## What's actually open

The plan's novelty sentence, "nobody closes the loop: frozen pi0.5 → extracted bank → composition → task success", is **false as written**. ReGuide (2609.38616) does exactly that loop: a frozen pi0.5, a bank derived post hoc from training demos, no target demos, and closed-loop LIBERO compositional success of 39 → 94.5. ReGuide's bank, however, is **external and geometric**. It stores end-effector pose statistics from the demos, uses object poses from an oracle or detector, and drives the arm with a scripted controller before handing back to the policy. It never reads parts out of the VLA's internals and never conditions the VLA differently.

What remains open:
1. A bank extracted **from the frozen model's own representation** (prefix K/V role tokens) and composed **inside the conditioning**, with no external grounding module and no scripted motion.
2. **Search plus verification** with a failure gate over such compositions.
3. A composition-space comparison: K/V vs velocity (CAG / RL²) vs weight (task vectors).

CRAFT shows K/V skill tokens are swap-stable (4.0±3.1%), but it trains them. The SAE work shows internal primitives exist in pi0.5, at 2.6–10.8% general features, and that pi0.5 survives hooking (≥95%). The OpenVLA failure does not carry over. This makes post-hoc internal extraction on pi0.5 plausible, and it removes the "SAE loses success" foil from the story.

Bars to beat:
- **ReGuide**: about 94.5 on its LIBERO cells, under oracle grounding. Add it as a mandatory baseline next to CAG-VA.
- **CAG**: code is public, and it scores 66.5 on CRAFT Pick-Place with pi0.5.

CRAFT's benchmarks are not released, so R5 cannot use them yet. REFACTOR-VLA's return-preservation gate is inert on LIBERO in its own paper, so citing it as a working template needs care.
